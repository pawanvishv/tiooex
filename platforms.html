Commerce

One system for the site, the store, the till, and the stock room

Commerce covers the online shop, the offline store, the point of sale, and the inventory those three draw from.

  • Online
  • Offline
  • POS
  • Inventory
Talk about commerce

The point

One catalog and one stock position. A sale on the site, in the shop, or at the till changes the same order.

How we use it

Staff sell, receive, and count from the same facts. The till talks to the warehouse, so nothing waits on a nightly file.

Where it helps

A customer is not promised stock the shelf no longer holds. A manager sees what sold and what still has to be picked.

Where it is used

Retail, online stores, warehouses, and counters. On the website, the shop floor, the POS, and in the stock room.

Mobility software

The vehicle, the trip, and the job that closes it

Mobility software follows a vehicle, a fleet, or a field asset from the trip to the person who finishes the work.

  • Fleet
  • Trip
  • Workshop job
  • Field record
Talk about mobility

The point

Follow the asset from the trip to a job someone can close. The road and the workshop share one record.

How we use it

Dispatch assigns the work. The field records what was done. The next person sees that record the same day.

Where it helps

A fault, a trip, or a job becomes a closed task. The operator and the workshop stop using yesterday’s status.

Where it is used

Automotive, logistics, field service, and energy crews. In the fleet yard, on the road, in the workshop, and at the dispatch desk.

Banking and insurance

The request, the product, and the case behind it

The customer request, the account or policy, and the claim or case stay on one path, with the control team on that path.

  • Account
  • Policy
  • Claim
  • Control evidence
Talk about banking

The point

The request, the account or policy, and the claim stay together, with the evidence the control team already needs.

How we use it

A case is opened, reviewed, and closed on one path. The same facts are not copied into a second system.

Where it helps

The customer, the product owner, and the control team work from the same case. A claim is not stuck in a queue the others cannot see.

Where it is used

Banks, insurers, and the teams behind an account or a policy. In the branch, the contact center, the claims desk, and with the control team.

Games

Tiooex Play

The games line covers the session a player is in, the account they return to, and the purchase around a title.

  • Live session
  • Player account
  • Catalog
  • Purchase
Talk about Tiooex Play

The point

The live session, the player account, and the purchase sit in one place, so a release can be operated, not only launched.

How we use it

The studio or publisher runs who is playing, what they bought, and what is live right now from the same platform.

Where it helps

A live event does not wait on three vendors to agree after the fact. The catalog and the session stay together.

Where it is used

Game studios, publishers, and live events. In the session, the player account, the catalog, and the purchase.
